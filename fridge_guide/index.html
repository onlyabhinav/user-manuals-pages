      What each button does

      Tap Freezer

      Freezer temperature

      Each press goes one step colder: -15 → -17 → -19 → -21 → -23 °C, then back to -15. Default -19 °C.

      ⚠Don’t use -15 °C in summer (above 35 °C). Don’t put glass bottles of liquid in the freezer: they crack when frozen.
      Tap Fridge

      Fridge temperature

      Each press goes one step colder: 7 → 4 → 3 → 2 → 1 °C → Vacation. Default 3 °C.

      ⚠Avoid 1 °C in winter and 5–7 °C in summer: both can hurt cooling.
      Hold Fridge 3 s

      Power Cool

      Runs the fan at full speed for several hours to chill new food fast, then returns to your setting. Hold 3 s again to stop early.

      Uses more electricity, so turn it off if you don’t need it.

      Tap Fridge past 1 °C

      Vacation mode

      For trips. The fridge is kept under 17 °C; the freezer keeps working. Press Fridge again to exit (goes to 7 °C).

      Empty the fridge compartment and close the door first.

      Hold Fridge 10 s

      Wi-Fi on / off

      The Wi-Fi icon blinks for 5 s, then stays on (or turns off). A blinking icon when you open the door means the fridge isn’t connected to the app yet.

      In the app

      AI Energy mode

      Turn on in SmartThings (Energy menu). A 🍃 icon shows on the panel while it runs. Saves energy but may reduce cooling a little.

      Freezer Convert

      Switch the freezer to Fridge mode

      1. Take all frozen food out of the freezer.
      2. Press and hold Freezer Convert for 3 seconds. The indicator blinks.
      3. Tap Freezer Convert until 💧 Fridge is lit.
      4. Wait 10 seconds. The change is applied.

      To switch back: take chilled food out, hold for 3 s, tap until Off (Freezer) is lit, and wait. The freezer restarts at -19 °C.

      ⛔Before you switch
      • Frozen food will thaw and spoil in Fridge mode.
      • Chilled food will freeze when you switch back.
      • Never leave glass bottles of liquid in the freezer: they can burst.
      ℹNormal after convertingDew may form on the inner walls as it warms up. Use plastic containers, especially for vegetables.

      Make ice

      Twist ice maker, some models only.

      1. Open the freezer door and slide out the ice tray.
      2. Fill with water up to the max line on the inner back of the tray.
      3. Slide the tray back in. Time to freeze depends on your temperature setting.
      4. Check the storage bin is in place, then turn the handle to the right to drop the cubes in.

      Pull the bin forward to take it out. If the ice maker doesn’t fit under the shelf, put it on the freezer shelf.

      SmartThings app

      Some models only. Lets you check and control the fridge from your phone.

      Connect your fridge

      1. Install SmartThings from Google Play, Galaxy Store or the App Store.
      2. Sign in with a Samsung account (the app helps you make one for free).
      3. Make sure the fridge’s Wi-Fi is on (hold Fridge 10 s).
      4. Open the app. When it says a new device was found, tap ADD NOW. If not, tap + and choose the fridge.
      5. Not listed? Tap Supported Devices → Refrigerator → your model.
      6. Follow the steps in the app. The fridge then shows as a card on your Devices screen.

      Works with 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (802.11 b/g/n; n recommended) and WPA/TKIP or WPA2/AES security.

      Fix it

      Pick what you’re noticing, then tick off each check. Most problems are fixed before you need to call service.

      Is this noise normal?

      All of these are normal. A service visit for a normal sound may be charged.

      🚗 Clicking, chirping or buzzing when it starts or stops

      The compressor starting or stopping, a bit like a car engine. It fades as the fridge settles.

      🌀 Whirring or “sssrrr” sounds

      The fan is running. It stops once the fridge reaches the set temperature.

      💨 Sizzling or hissing

      During defrosting, water drips onto the defrost heater.

      🫖 Bubbling or gurgling

      Refrigerant flowing through the sealed pipes as it cools.

      🪵 Cracking or knocking

      Plastic parts shrinking and expanding as the temperature changes, mostly during defrost.

      🐝 Buzzing (ice maker models)

      The water valve opening to fill the ice maker.

      🚪 Whooshing when you open or close the door

      Air pressure evening out.

      Power cut

      • Ask your electricity company how long it will last.
      • Cuts of an hour or two won’t affect the temperature much. Open the doors as little as possible.
      • If the power is off for more than 24 hours, throw out all frozen food.

      Care & cleaning

      How to take parts out, clean safely and save energy.

      Take parts out

      Shelves (fridge & freezer)

      1. Hold with both hands.
      2. Lift to free it from the rear hook.
      3. Tilt to one side and pull out.

      To put it back, check the shape of the back edge and reverse the steps.

      ⚠Shelves are heavy. Never insert one upside down or back to front: it could drop.

      Vegetable drawer & shelf

      Drawer: gently lift the front and slide it out.

      Shelf above it: hold with both hands, lift off the rear hook, tilt and pull out.

      Door bins

      1. Empty the bin.
      2. Press gently on its sides.
      3. Lift it straight up.
      ⚠Always empty bins first to avoid accidents.
      ℹAfter cleaning, check the parts map to make sure everything is back in the right place.

      Cleaning

      Regular cleaning

      1. Unplug the fridge.
      2. Wipe inside and out with a damp, soft, lint-free cloth or paper towel. Mild detergent in warm water is fine.
      3. Dry well with a dry cloth.
      4. Wipe dust off the plug pins with a dry cloth, then plug back in.

      Never use

      • Benzene, thinner, bleach (Clorox), or car/home detergents
      • Abrasive cleaners, window sprays, waxes or flammable fluids
      • Water sprayed directly onto the fridge
      • Warm water on cold glass shelves: they can crack
      • A hair dryer to dry inside, or candles to remove smells

      Inside light

      ⛔LED lamps aren’t user-replaceableDon’t try to change them yourself (risk of electric shock). Contact a Samsung service centre.

      Save energy

      • Keep it in a cool, dry, well-ventilated room, away from sunlight and heaters.
      • Don’t block vents or grilles.
      • Let warm food cool down before putting it in.
      • Thaw frozen food in the fridge: it helps keep the fridge cold.
      • Close the door quickly. Shorter openings also mean less frost.
      • Leave space at the back and sides.
      • Keep baskets, drawers and shelves where they came from the factory.

      The fridge is frost-free: it defrosts itself. Don’t refreeze food that has fully thawed.

      The most important warnings from the manual, grouped. The printed manual has the full list.

      🔥 Refrigerant gas & gas leaks

      This fridge may use isobutane (R-600a): environmentally friendly but flammable. Check the label on the back or inside.

      • Never damage the refrigerant pipes, especially when moving or installing.
      • Don’t store flammable things (gas, alcohol, benzene, aerosols) in or near it.
      ⛔If you suspect a gas leakKeep flames and sparks away, air the room for several minutes, don’t touch the plug or any switches, and call service.
      🔌 Power cord & socket
      • Plug into its own earthed wall socket that matches the rating label.
      • Keep plug pins clean and dry.
      • Unplug before cleaning or maintenance.
      • Multi-plug power strips, or loose wall sockets
      • Bending, twisting, tying or crushing the cord
      • Pushing the cord behind the fridge
      • Touching the plug with wet hands
      • Using a cracked or damaged cord: have service replace it
      📍 Where to put it
      • Damp places or where it can get wet
      • Direct sunlight, or near stoves and heaters
      • Covering the ventilation openings
      🧒 Children
      • Don’t let children play with it, hang on the door, climb in or stand on drawers.
      • Keep fingers away from door gaps (pinch points).
      • Keep packaging, especially plastic bags, away from children.
      ⛔Throwing out an old fridge?Take off the doors and seals so a child can’t get trapped inside. Leave the shelves in place.
      🥫 Everyday use
      • Don’t overfill: things can fall out when you open the door.
      • Don’t put containers of water on top of the fridge.
      • Don’t store medicines or anything that needs exact temperatures.
      • Don’t use electrical appliances inside unless the manufacturer approves them.
      • Don’t use tools to speed up defrosting.
      • Don’t put your hands under the bottom of the fridge: there are sharp edges.
      ⛔Smell burning or smoke, or water gets inside? Unplug it at once and call service.
      ♻️ Disposal

      Don’t throw it out with household waste. Recycle the packaging and the fridge separately, and make sure the pipes on the back aren’t damaged. For collection, call 1800 40 7267864 or visit samsung.com/in/support.
